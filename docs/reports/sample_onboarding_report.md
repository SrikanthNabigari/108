## ✦ Your Challenges

### Mangal Dosha — Mars in the 8th House

Mars in your 8th house creates what's called Mangal Dosha. In traditional terms, this affects marriage and partnerships. But here's what it actually means for you:

You experience relationships intensely. Half-measures don't work. You're either all-in or you're out. This can create friction with partners who prefer a lighter touch. Mars is also retrograde and debilitated (in Cancer) — meaning your anger turns inward rather than outward. You process conflict internally, which can manifest as anxiety or health issues in the stomach/chest area.

**The reality:** Mangal Dosha is common (~40% of charts have it) and doesn't doom your relationships. It means you need a partner who can match your intensity.

### Sun-Rahu Conjunction (Grahan Dosha) — 12th House

Your Sun sits with Rahu in the 12th house (Scorpio). This creates a subtle but persistent pattern: **you may struggle to feel recognized for who you truly are.** Authority figures (father, bosses) may have been complicated. There's a pull toward foreign lands, isolation, or work behind the scenes.

The flip side? This combination gives you extraordinary ability to research hidden things, work with foreign connections, and access spiritual dimensions that most people can't touch.
