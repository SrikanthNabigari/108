## ⚡ This Week's Key Alert

### Mars Conjunct Your Natal Saturn — Feb 9-11

The biggest transit event this week: **Mars at Capricorn 19-20° crossing your natal Saturn at 19°56'.** Exact on Feb 10.

This happens once every ~2 years. It's not dangerous — but it's pressure. Saturn is your 2nd house ruler (money, speech, family) AND 3rd house ruler (courage, siblings, communication). Mars activating it means:

- **Finances:** An unexpected expense, a bill, or a money decision that feels forced
- **Speech:** You might say something sharper than you intend. Or someone says something sharp to you
- **Work:** A task that requires extreme patience and discipline — the kind that tests you
- **Physical:** Increased body heat, possible headache or muscle tension. Exercise helps

**The upside:** Mars-Saturn transits reward disciplined action. If there's something you've been procrastinating on — especially something difficult — this transit gives you the steel to push through it.
