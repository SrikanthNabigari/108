## 2. The Samkhya Framework — 25 Tattvas

The entire Swara system rests on the Samkhya evolutionary framework. The 25 Tattvas describe how consciousness manifests into matter:

```
PURUSHA (Consciousness / Observer)
    │
PRAKRITI (Unmanifest Nature — 3 Gunas in equilibrium)
    │
    ├── MAHAT / BUDDHI (Cosmic Intelligence / Discernment)
    │
    ├── AHAMKARA (Ego / "I-maker")
    │       │
    │       ├── SATTVIC branch → MANAS (Mind) + 5 Jnanendriyas + 5 Karmendriyas
    │       ├── TAMASIC branch → 5 Tanmatras → 5 Mahabhutas
    │       └── RAJASIC branch → Provides energy to both above
    │
    ├── MANAS (Sensory Mind)
    │
    ├── 5 JNANENDRIYAS (Sense organs):
    │       Hearing, Touch, Sight, Taste, Smell
    │
    ├── 5 KARMENDRIYAS (Action organs):
    │       Speech, Grasping, Movement, Reproduction, Excretion
    │
    ├── 5 TANMATRAS (Subtle elements):
    │       Shabda (Sound), Sparsha (Touch), Rupa (Form),
    │       Rasa (Taste), Gandha (Smell)
    │
    └── 5 MAHABHUTAS (Gross elements):
            Akasha (Ether), Vayu (Air), Agni (Fire),
            Jala (Water), Prithvi (Earth)
```

**Key insight for computation:** The 5 Mahabhutas are NOT independent — each contains the Tanmatras of all elements above it:
- Akasha → has only Sound
- Vayu → has Sound + Touch
- Agni → has Sound + Touch + Form
- Jala → has Sound + Touch + Form + Taste
- Prithvi → has Sound + Touch + Form + Taste + Smell

This is why Prithvi (Earth) is the most "dense" — it contains all 5 sensory qualities. Akasha is the most subtle — it contains only one.

### 4.3 Tattva Cycle Within a Hora (Shiva Swarodaya)

Within each Nadi period (~60 minutes), all 5 Tattvas cycle in descending density order:

```
├── Prithvi (Earth) ─── 20 min (33.3%) ─── Most dense, most stable
├── Jala (Water)    ─── 16 min (26.7%) ─── Flowing, adaptable
├── Agni (Fire)     ─── 12 min (20.0%) ─── Transformative, sharp
├── Vayu (Air)      ───  8 min (13.3%) ─── Mobile, dispersing
└── Akasha (Ether)  ───  4 min  (6.7%) ─── Most subtle, transcendent
    ──────────────────── 60 min (100%)
```

**Scaling for variable Hora duration:** Since day horas and night horas have different lengths:
```
actual_tattva_duration = (tattva_proportion) × (actual_hora_duration)

Example: Summer day hora = 75 minutes
  Prithvi = 75 × 20/60 = 25.0 min
  Jala    = 75 × 16/60 = 20.0 min
  Agni    = 75 × 12/60 = 15.0 min
  Vayu    = 75 × 8/60  = 10.0 min
  Akasha  = 75 × 4/60  =  5.0 min
```

**Important note:** Some texts describe equal 12-minute cycles (60/5). The 20-16-12-8-4 sequence from the Bihar School commentary is the most widely cited and follows the logic of decreasing density = decreasing duration. We use this as our primary model.

## 6. Shukla/Krishna Paksha — Nadi Activation Rules

### 6.1 Starting Nadi by Tithi (from Shiva Swarodaya, verses 62-63)

The Nadi that should be active at SUNRISE is determined by the lunar day (Tithi):

**SHUKLA PAKSHA (Bright/Waxing Fortnight — Pratipada to Purnima):**

| Tithis | Starting Nadi at Sunrise |
|--------|------------------------|
| 1, 2, 3 (Pratipada, Dwitiya, Tritiya) | **Ida (Left / Chandra)** |
| 4, 5, 6 (Chaturthi, Panchami, Shashthi) | **Pingala (Right / Surya)** |
| 7, 8, 9 (Saptami, Ashtami, Navami) | **Ida (Left / Chandra)** |
| 10, 11, 12 (Dashami, Ekadashi, Dwadashi) | **Pingala (Right / Surya)** |
| 13, 14, 15 (Trayodashi, Chaturdashi, Purnima) | **Ida (Left / Chandra)** |

**KRISHNA PAKSHA (Dark/Waning Fortnight — Pratipada to Amavasya):**

| Tithis | Starting Nadi at Sunrise |
|--------|------------------------|
| 1, 2, 3 | **Pingala (Right / Surya)** |
| 4, 5, 6 | **Ida (Left / Chandra)** |
| 7, 8, 9 | **Pingala (Right / Surya)** |
| 10, 11, 12 | **Ida (Left / Chandra)** |
| 13, 14, 15 | **Pingala (Right / Surya)** |

**Pattern:** Alternating in blocks of 3 Tithis. Krishna Paksha is the MIRROR of Shukla Paksha.

### 6.2 Daily Nadi Alternation

After the starting Nadi is established at sunrise:
- Each Nadi runs for approximately **60 minutes** (1 Hora equivalent)
- Then switches to the opposite Nadi for 60 minutes
- During the **1-4 minute transition**, Sushumna is briefly active
- This creates **24 alternations** in a 24-hour period (12 Ida + 12 Pingala)

### 6.3 Mismatch Detection (Health Warning from Swarodaya)

If the ACTUAL active Nadi does NOT match the PREDICTED Nadi:
- **Occasional mismatch:** Normal variation, no concern
- **Persistent mismatch (same Nadi active for 3+ hours):** Health warning — disease may be incubating
- **Continuous single-Nadi flow for full day:** Serious health concern — seek medical attention
- **Continuous Sushumna for extended period:** Spiritual transformation OR medical emergency

## 9. Scientific Validation

### 9.1 The Nasal Cycle (Modern Science Confirms)

Modern science has validated the core physiological claim of the Shiva Swarodaya:

**Confirmed findings:**
- **Ultradian nasal cycle exists:** Alternating congestion/decongestion of nasal passages
- **Mean cycle period:** ~85.4 minutes (close to the traditional 60-90 minute claim)
- **Range:** 25 minutes to 8 hours (follows chaotic, not perfectly regular, rhythm)
- **Mechanism:** Hypothalamus-regulated autonomic control of erectile tissue in nasal passages

### 9.2 Brain Hemisphere Correlation

**EEG studies confirm (Nature Scientific Reports, 2021):**
- Right nostril dominance → Enhanced LEFT hemisphere activity (verbal, analytical, logical)
- Left nostril dominance → Enhanced RIGHT hemisphere activity (spatial, creative, intuitive)
- The contralateral hemisphere shows greater EEG amplitude across all frequency bands

**This directly maps to the Swarodaya claims:**
- Pingala (Right/Sun) → Active, analytical, assertive = Left brain
- Ida (Left/Moon) → Receptive, creative, intuitive = Right brain

### 9.3 Autonomic Nervous System Coupling

- **Right nostril (Pingala):** Sympathetic activation (fight-or-flight, alertness, arousal)
- **Left nostril (Ida):** Parasympathetic activation (rest-and-digest, calm, recovery)
- **Both nostrils (Sushumna):** Balanced autonomic state

### 9.4 Limitations and Caveats

- Causal relationship between nasal dominance and cognitive tasks remains debated
- Cycle timing is chaotic (not perfectly predictable by planetary position alone)
- Disease prediction accuracy is not scientifically validated
- Individual variation is significant
- The functional significance of nasal laterality is still under investigation (Thaploo et al., 2024)
