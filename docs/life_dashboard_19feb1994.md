Your Mahadasha lord (Rahu) sits in the 8th house. The 8th house is:

For 18 years (2011-2029), every experience gets filtered through this lens. Even good things come with a twist. A job opportunity has a hidden catch. A relationship has an undisclosed dimension. Money arrives from unexpected sources and leaves for unexpected reasons.

- Your ideas are brilliant but they get overshadowed — by your own ego, by authority figures, or by circumstances
- Communication doesn't land the way you intend

- You have the capacity for wealth (exalted 2nd house Moon)

- There's a persistent feeling that something is missing — even when objectively things are fine

**This dissolves over time.** Ketu's influence lessens as you age and integrate its spiritual lessons. By your late 30s, you'll be able to hold both — material success AND inner peace.
